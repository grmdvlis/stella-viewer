City labels use offline point-in-polygon lookup. Coordinates are never sent to a geocoding provider.
Source: Statistics Korea SGIS (통계청 통계지리정보서비스 제공), 2013 municipality boundaries.
GeoJSON conversion / simplification: https://github.com/southkorea/southkorea-maps
Original file: kostat/2013/json/skorea_municipalities_geo.json
Local simplification: topology-preserving tolerance 0.0005 degrees, coordinates rounded to five decimal places.
Source license statement: korea-cities-license.png (archived SGIS reply, 2013).
These simplified, historical boundaries provide approximate city labels; boundary changes and coastline simplification can affect results. GPS latitude/longitude are displayed independently at three decimal places.
Outside South Korea: city unavailable, except the explicitly selected Sydney preset.
